How I built this site for INR 667

This site, brand system included, cost INR 667 to build. Not a typo, not a marketing line. That's the actual number, and I want to walk through how it got that low without the output looking like it.

The old way versus this way

The old way was hiring a designer for the visual system, a developer for the build, and paying for stock photography or a shoot. Weeks of back and forth, a budget in the lakhs, and a site that's expensive to change six months later when the positioning shifts.

This way was writing a brand brief myself, generating and refining the visual system with AI tools, writing every line of copy in my own voice, and having AI handle the code. The whole thing ran through the same daily stack I already use for client work: Claude for structure and copy, Canva for supporting visual assets, and Codex for the parts of the build that needed direct code changes.

Where the money actually went

Domain registration (mishrawrites.com)INR 599
Font and asset licensing checksINR 68
HostingINR 0 (GitHub Pages)
TotalINR 667

Everything else, the brand guidelines, the copywriting, the page layouts, the code, ran on tools I already pay for as part of my monthly stack, so the marginal cost of this specific project was close to zero.

What I'd tell someone trying this themselves

Write the brand brief before you touch any tool. Color, type, voice, what you will and won't say, decided on paper first. Every AI tool works better against a brief than against a blank prompt. The brief is the leverage, not the tool.

Second, keep a human editing pass on every piece of copy. AI drafts fast; it does not know your actual wins, your actual numbers, or the difference between confident and overstated. That judgment stayed mine through every page on this site.
