    About Gaurav Mishra

    Research first, numbers always.

    I started in 2014 as a primary researcher, sitting across from people and asking what they really thought of the brands they bought. I never stopped asking.

    At H&R Block I turned tax, a subject nobody wants to think about, into content a country searched for. At Ketto I made it easier for families to ask for help. At FITTR I took a six-pack brand to wellbeing for everyone and grew a community across 70+ countries. At TransPerfect Paybooks I moved a payroll brand upmarket and built an AI engine that finds buyers in seven markets every day.

    The method doesn't change: find the gap between how a brand sees itself and how its customers see it, then close it with creative, content and community that scale. I'm ready for the next full-time role where I can do that at a bigger scale.
