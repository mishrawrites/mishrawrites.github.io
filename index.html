What I Solve

Three problems that show up on a hiring leader's desk.

01

Positioning that doesn't match how revenue gets made

The product is strong, the story isn't. ICP is fuzzy, pricing feels apologetic, and sales rewrites the pitch on every call because marketing hasn't given them one that holds.

02

Campaigns nobody can run again

A campaign ships, it works, and then it's gone: no playbook, no content engine, no process the next hire can pick up. Every quarter starts from zero.

03

Marketing spend with no line to revenue

Brand work and the P&L live in separate decks. Nobody can point to which content, campaign, or pricing shift actually moved MRR, ARR, or CAC.
