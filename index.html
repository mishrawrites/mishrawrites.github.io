What I Solve

Three problems I'm brought in to fix.

01

A brand people see differently from its founders

The product is strong, and the story has drifted from it. Founders describe one brand and buyers describe another. I start with research that names that gap, then build the insight the brand can own. At FITTR, that turned a six-pack brand into preventive health and wellbeing for everyone.

02

Creative that needs a sharper brief

Films, ads and posts ship, and the audience scrolls on. I map every design and copy choice to what the audience actually needs, so the insight carries through performance ads, brand films, TV, OTT, cinema, billboards and the street.

03

Growth that leans on paid media

Paid reach gets expensive fast. I build the social, content and community engines that keep acquiring at a fraction of the cost. At FITTR, community acquired users at $6 each, against $47 on paid performance.
