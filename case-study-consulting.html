Brand foundations for three founders

Independent brand strategy across a wellness brand, a kitchen-and-home brand moving into food storage, and a family-wealth fintech app.

Summary

I gave three founders the brand foundation they needed to grow on. I worked as the sole brand strategist on every mandate, from research and positioning through naming, identity and launch groundwork.

Mountainor

May 2023 to December 2023

An emerging health and personal care brand. Over a six-month mandate I built its brand foundation and wellness positioning, visual identity, packaging and tone of voice, then set up performance marketing, influencer outreach and a resell funnel on WhatsApp.

Packaging was part of this brief, the same lever I wrote about in I'm A Complan Buoy.

Crystal Life

January 2025 to December 2025

A kitchen and home essentials brand moving into food storage, up against Gladware and Ziplock. I ran primary research across major metros, identified the storage-container category opportunity, named the range Podware and the product Pod, and led positioning. Podware is due to launch in 2027.

FAMLI

July 2025 to December 2025

A fintech app bringing family-office style financial planning to Indian families. I built its brand foundation and positioning. The app reached 1,000+ active users in a three-month beta.
