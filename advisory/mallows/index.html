Mallows

A sauces and spreads brand whose quality products were held back by local sticker labels. The studio team designed printed packs across retail jars (Schezwan chutney, green chilli sauce, mango jam) and a 5 kg ketchup can for restaurants and caterers, with food photography doing the heavy lifting, plus marketing collateral, a documentary video on how the products are made, a website with online ordering, retail branding and influencer work.
