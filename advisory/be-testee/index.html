Pantry · Pickles and sauces

Be Testee

Be Testee is a Pune food brand from Jaiswal's Brothers, who had the recipes but no brand. Positioning, story and go-to-market were shaped with consumer research behind them. The studio team created the name, the identity and packs for four pickles and four sauces that sell the occasion: an illustrated mango harvest on the pickle jar, friends sharing pizza on the sauce sachet.
