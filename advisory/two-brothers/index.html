Two Brothers India Farms

Two Brothers India Farms is an ECOCERT-certified organic farm in Bhodani, Pune district, known for cultured ghee from free-grazed Gir cows. The work covered positioning and pack-size pricing, the farm story the label tells, and how the ghee shows up online: marketplace and quick-commerce listings, product content and influencer work, grounded in consumer research. The studio team carried this into the label, with the farm story placed where the eye lands first.
