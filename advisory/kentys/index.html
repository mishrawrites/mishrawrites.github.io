Kenty's

Kenty's, from Kuch Agro, sells chips, extruded snacks, namkeen, noodles and carbonated drinks, with roots in the Kutch region. The work set one system for a fast-growing range, including new launches. The studio team designed the logo and the packs around a chef mascot and a red script logo, from a kids' ketchup moon chips pack to PET bottles for cola and jeera masala soda, and built the website with recipes, van design, marketing collateral and influencer work with food bloggers.
