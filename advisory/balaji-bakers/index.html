Bakery · Bakery and namkeen, 25 years

Balaji Bakers

A bakery and namkeen maker from Chalkaranji (Ichalkaranji) in Kolhapur district, marking 25 years, that wanted to move its old packaging into a premium segment. The work set one master brand for a range that runs from Kolhapuri bhadang to pav, buns, bread and cupcakes, and took it beyond the pack. The studio team designed a new logo and every pack around the red Balaji badge and the 25-year seal, then carried it into franchise outlets, the bakery space, marketing collateral, social media, influencers, festive campaigns, radio jingles and newspaper ads.
