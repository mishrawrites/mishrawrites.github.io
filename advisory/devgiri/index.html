Devgiri

Devgiri is the cooperative milk union of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad) district. The brief was to make a cooperative's range speak as one consumer brand, with one message order across milk, lassi and toned milk and the ISO 22000 credential up front. The studio team designed the packs and turned the delivery van into a moving shelf for the whole range, a story that also carried into communication for distributors.
