Dairy · New dairy brand, 23 packs

OONAS

A client entering milk and milk products, with operations planned but no brand. The studio presented six names and the client chose OONAS. The studio team then built the identity, a marketing strategy and 23 packs across milk, shrikhand, amrakhand, lassi, buttermilk, yogurt, flavoured milk, pedha, khova and butter, all built on transparency, with the campaign line Sahi bhi and Real bhi.
