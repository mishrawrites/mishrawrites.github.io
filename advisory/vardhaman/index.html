Bakery · Jain whole-grain bakery

Vardhaman Bakery

Vardhaman is a pure vegetarian, 100% Jain bakery using whole wheat, jaggery, jowar and nachani. Its plain, transparent packs did not show that quality, which held back growth. The studio team created a new trademarked identity and packaging that puts the grains up front, then carried it into outdoor media, a product photoshoot, social media and a website with an online store.
