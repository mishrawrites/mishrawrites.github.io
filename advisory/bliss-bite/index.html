Bliss Bite

A local food business that sold snacks with basic labels, turned into a beverage brand. The studio team created the name and identity, packs for four flavours (strawberry, orange, mango and lemon) with a colour each, and retail branding from standees, posters and hoardings to van and shop boards, plus tasting events in residential societies.
