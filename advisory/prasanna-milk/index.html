Prasanna Milk

Prasanna's dairy line spans daily buffalo milk, a ghee range, a chocolate flavoured milk called Uplift and modak for Ganeshotsav. The work set the positioning and the story behind the range, and what each pack had to say. The studio team gave each format its own job: the milk pouch carries the purity claims, Uplift talks to kids, and the modak box borrows festival red and gold.
