Packs that have to win on a crowded shelf.

Regional food and dairy brands, most of them family-run and based in Maharashtra, have brought me in to help their packs compete with national brands on the same shelf. The work is deciding what the pack has to say, to whom and in what order, then making a whole range hold together. Each page has a short brief and the products I led.
