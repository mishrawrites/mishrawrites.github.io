Summary

H&R Block India had deep tax expertise in a category people need once a year and approach with caution. I built the content engine that carried that expertise to people: a video studio in an unused meeting room and a long-form Knowledge Center. Organic traffic went from under 50K to 30M a season, and the Knowledge Center grew into a revenue channel.

The challenge

The category

Tax is high-need and low-love. Trust is fragile and interest peaks for one season a year.

The opportunity

Convert tax complexity into approachable content and campaign hooks, so people find H&R Block when the question arrives.

What I built

  1. From 2015

    The Content Lab

    I converted an unused meeting room into a studio and publishing engine, and scaled it to a team of 4 plus 20 interns. It produced 500+ videos, at least 2 to 3 a day and up to 10 on peak days.

  2. 2015 to 2018

    The Knowledge Center

    Long-form SEO infrastructure with 500+ articles that turned tax education into an acquisition channel. It reached 50%+ category search share by 2018 and brought in roughly 15% of clients and 25% of revenue.

  3. Social

    Find a Flaw

    A participative content hook built around spotting tax mistakes. Facebook followers grew 10 to 20% month on month and LinkedIn 30% month on month, and engagement kept climbing after the rewards were removed.

  4. Social

    Taxventures with Narad Muni

    A social media property recognized by DIGIXX with its Social Awareness award.

Campaigns and films

Alongside the always-on content, I ran India's brand campaigns end to end, including Tax Samasya Unlock with H&R Block, #BeTheBoss and the Tax Pledge. Over nearly five years, my India campaigns and content reached 200M+ people.

Every film is on H&R Block India's YouTube channel.

Attribution

Organic growth is directly attributable to the Knowledge Center and Content Lab infrastructure I built and ran in-house. Every video, campaign and film on H&R Block India's YouTube channel is my work.

The wider season engine

The content ran alongside a full-season marketing operation I led for India: marketing and branding for 8 retail offices, season budgets of $1.2M to $1.5M across digital and offline media, including tech parks, billboards, radio, cinema, booths, events and flash mobs, and communications across 800+ alliances with banks, financial institutions and job portals. I also managed expat clients through referral, retention and loyalty programs.

Global campaign work

I contributed to creative for the US tax season and to global campaigns, including Get Your Taxes Won with Jon Hamm for the Super Bowl, working with agency Fallon. My part: creative adaptations, media vehicle planning, spend monitoring and impact tracking.
