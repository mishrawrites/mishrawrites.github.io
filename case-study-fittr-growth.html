Summary

When I joined FITTR in March 2020, the product was already strong: 200K people trusted its coaches with their health. It needed a marketing engine and a story big enough to reach people outside that community. I built the team, took the Transformation Challenge beyond the community for the first time, and repositioned the brand through its first brand film. The community grew to 800K+ members across 70+ countries, and every new app user and download came through the engine my team ran.

The community was the asset. The marketing team and its functions were still to be built, so I built them: performance marketing, creative, content, CRM and in-app engagement, each with its own owner.

The insight

In one line

Transformation identity beats fitness features.

The creative brief I wrote for FITTR's brand film defined the audience as a top-of-funnel user with zero awareness of FITTR: the sometimer, the imperfect fitter. That person buys into who they could become. So the work led with transformation stories and challenge rituals, with features second.

The full strategy, from segmentation to the rebrand, has its own page: making fitness fun, approachable and easy.

What I built

  1. From 2020

    The team and its functions

    I set up performance marketing, creative, content and in-app engagement, established the CRM team and brought in CleverTap to measure engagement and retention, then kicked off cross-sell and upsell initiatives. The team grew to 16.

  2. Seasons 10 to 18

    The Transformation Challenge, taken outside the community

    FITTR's 12-week challenge ran three times a year. I scaled it with content, community rituals, engagement loops and a campaign line for every season, and opened it to people outside the community for the first time. It reached 45M+ people cumulatively, drove 2M+ app downloads and accounted for roughly 20% of customer acquisition.

  3. During COVID

    FITTR Online PT

    Training from home during COVID, expanded into yoga, physiotherapy, rehab, HIIT and meditation. It reached 20%+ of total revenue with the highest international adoption in the portfolio, added 300K+ app downloads and lifted engagement 45%.

  4. Ongoing

    Content and utility libraries

    1M+ food entries, 10K+ recipes in three months and 1,000+ workout videos gave the community reasons to return daily. I scoped the workout library by format: gym and home resistance training, HIIT follow-alongs, yoga, rehab and prehab, and warm-ups. DAU rose 40%, MAU 25%, and in-app engagement 340% against baseline.

  5. Every year

    Events at every scale

    One mega event, 3 to 5 medium events and 30+ localized mini events a year. The biggest was Connect Fest in 2022, a $636K festival with Milind Soman and Mandira Bedi.

  6. Launch year

    FITTR HART

    I gave product input and wrote the India GTM for FITTR's smart ring, supported by high-visibility creators and athletes. It made $900K in year one, with sales growing 7× month on month.

  7. 2022

    Fitness Made Forever

    FITTR's integrated brand campaign across TV, OTT, print, cinema, billboards, Meta, Google and global influencer networks, on a $1.3M budget. It reached 100M+ people.
