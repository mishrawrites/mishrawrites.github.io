Summary

GST changed how every business in India filed tax, and businesses needed clarity fast. H&R Block India had the expertise. I turned it into an education series people kept referencing, then used the trust it built to launch earlyGST, a B2B SaaS for GST filing.

What I built

  1. Education first

    GST Made Simple

    A 40+ episode video series produced in the Content Lab, explaining the transition in plain language. It drew 300+ external references.

  2. Product

    earlyGST launch

    I launched earlyGST, a B2B SaaS for GST filing, and ran its GTM. 500+ businesses onboarded in the first 12 months.
