FITSHOP: building FITTR's store, and the call to close it

I helped turn FITTR's in-app rewards store into a full e-commerce store, ran it through 2021 and 2022, and recommended closing it once the numbers showed it would never match the core business.

The numbers

The store worked as a store. Members bought, conversion sat in the usual e-commerce range, and merchandise carried healthy gross margins. Paid acquisition was the problem: ads returned less than it cost to win a first order.

Starting point

The insight came from FITSHOP

Members earned FITCOINS by contributing to the app and spent them on FITTR goods. What they picked showed real demand for FITTR-branded gear.

FITTR's values ruled out supplements, so the range stayed with merchandise: tees, hoodies, shakers, resistance bands, gym bags and home workout setups.

What we built

We gave the store everything a real e-commerce business needs, inside the FITTR app and website.

  1. Strategy

    A native store

    I helped turn FITSHOP from a rewards counter into a native store, so members could buy where they already spent their time.

  2. Assortment and pricing

    Merchandise only

    I set the range and the prices, and launched merchandise bundles to lift order value.

  3. Promotions

    Offers that moved the basket

    Free shipping above a threshold pushed customers toward bigger orders.

  4. Marketing

    Community first, then ads

    The store drew on FITTR's community and in-app channels, with paid ads on top to grow beyond them.

The call

What the store wasA store that paid for itself at small scale
What I recommendedClose it and back coachingInvestors and management wanted FITTR focused on coaching and the app
  • Coaching carried far higher margins than merchandise, so every rupee and every hour on the store competed with the core business.
  • Paid ads returned 1.5x to 2x where a first order needed about 2.5x to break even.
  • I recommended closing the store and moving the best-selling ideas into nutrition-plus-coaching bundles.

What carried forward

The bundles that took the store's learnings lifted ARPU on nutrition plans by more than 50%. See FITTR Online PT.

FITTR HART

Before I left in May 2023, I wrote the India GTM, the brand foundation and the e-commerce setup for FITTR's smart ring. It launched in February 2024, after my tenure, and the founder shared that HART crossed $100K in gross sales in a single month that February.
