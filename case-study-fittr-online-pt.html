FITTR Online PT: from a COVID idea to 24% of revenue

I co-created FITTR's Online Personal Training with the strategy head and owned it from idea to scale: a coach marketplace that put trainers who lost work in COVID in front of clients across the world.

The numbers

Online PT went from an idea in the first months of COVID to a quarter of FITTR's revenue, most of it earned abroad, on unit economics that paid back in about three months.

After my time

The international engine kept compounding. In November 2024, FITTR's founder, Jitendra Chouksey, shared publicly that "Fittr's online PT gets 90% of its clients from outside India", while FITTR's fitness and nutrition offering had historically been 70% domestic. Read the post ↗

The problem

In 2020, COVID shut gyms and left thousands of personal trainers without work. At the same time, people stuck at home wanted guided training and had no one to train them. FITTR had a large, trusting community and a strong nutrition coaching business, and no way yet to offer live training.

The idea

The mission

Bring personal trainers who lost their work online and connect them with FITTR's community. Save their livelihoods, build jobs for the fitness industry, and make online the future of personal training.

What I did

  1. Business model

    Built it and pitched it

    I co-created the offering with the strategy head and built the business model around it. I pitched it to the founder and then to investors at Sequoia Capital, with an ecosystem simulation for potential markets covering target personas, revenue systems, revenue estimates, profit projections, runway and evolution points.

  2. Product

    The buyer journey

    I worked with the product team to build the interface and buyer journey by August 2020, and linked Online PT with in-app engagement, which opened up cross-selling and upselling with nutrition plans.

  3. July 2020

    Launch

    I took the idea public through the founder's channels first, then a press note. Posts in the FITTR Facebook group and on LinkedIn went viral, and 500 clients joined the waitlist in about a month, all from the FITTR community, before any ads ran.

  4. Coach supply

    From 20 coaches to 100+

    With HR, I onboarded the first 20 personal trainers within two weeks; they had applied after seeing the founder's post. I set the SOP for hiring and training new coaches and coordinated with INFS, FITTR's sister concern, so every coach was properly trained. The bench grew to 100+ by March 2023, about 20% of them outside India.

  5. Portfolio and pricing

    Plans for every kind of client

    We started with strength training, then added yoga, pranayam, HIIT, Zumba, martial arts and other formats. I built plans by coach seniority, coaching type (1-on-1, family, groups of 3 or 5), domain and sessions per month. Nutrition-plus-PT bundles lifted ARPU on nutrition plans by more than 50%.

  6. International

    Developed markets first

    I targeted the US, Australia, the Middle East, Singapore and Europe, and onboarded foreign coaches to serve foreign clients. By FY 2022-23, 60% to 70% of PT revenue came from outside India.
