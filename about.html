About

Twelve years, five companies, one throughline.

I started my career as a primary researcher at SalesNXT, running one-on-one and group video studies and survey research for brands across major economies. A few of those research conversations turned into clients, which took me into sales, and from there into marketing. Research is still where every project of mine begins.

The question I keep coming back to is the gap between how founders think their brand is seen and how buyers actually see it. I find that gap, build the insight from it, set the design and copy direction that serves it, and carry it across every touchpoint until it scales. I've done it at H&R Block, Ketto, FITTR and TransPerfect Paybooks, and with the founders I consult for. The case studies show how.
